# Feature Specification: AI-First Vocabulary Curation

**Feature Branch**: `003-vocabulary-curation`
**Created**: 2026-10-03
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "Build an AI-first vocabulary curation system for the Indonesian–Malaysian Malay Anki deck with independent verification, evidence, adversarial review, conservative acceptance, repeatable batches, and a meaningful initial corpus."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-03

- Q: What minimum external evidence should an ordinary candidate have before automatic acceptance? → A: At least two independent, claim-relevant sources or signals support the key meaning and usage claims; unresolved disputes still block acceptance.
- Q: Should a false-friend card show the misleading lookalike and its actual meaning on the back? → A: Yes; show a concise, evidence-backed contrast on the back while keeping the front neutral.
- Q: What extra proof should a false-friend candidate need beyond the ordinary two-signal evidence gate? → A: Independent evidence must support each language's meaning and the misleading contrast, and separate verification and adversarial review must agree.

### Session 2026-10-05

- The learner deck is contrast-only. A concept whose normal Indonesian and Malaysian Malay forms have effectively identical spelling, meaning, and ordinary usage is excluded, even if it is frequent and well evidenced. `same` remains a curation classification for explaining rejection, not a learner relation. For example, `water → air / air` is excluded.

### Session 2026-10-12

Ordinary low-risk cards may use a shorter evidence-backed path with independent verification; adversarial review is mandatory for elevated risk and false friends, not for every ordinary card. Missing evidence, model hesitation, and exhausted research budgets are not affirmative grounds for rejection. Existing candidate and evidence history must remain reusable and versioned.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Curate a Trustworthy Batch (Priority: P1)

A maintainer selects a practical topic. AI discovers candidate concepts, reuses relevant inspected evidence, verifies claims independently, and accepts well-supported ordinary contrasts through a bounded fast path. Material risks trigger targeted escalation. Routine entries do not require item-by-item human review.

**Why this priority**: AI is the main growth mechanism, so the process must protect learners while producing accepted cards faster and with less repeated work than the first pipeline.

**Independent Test**: Curate a bounded batch containing ordinary pairs, an unsupported usage difference, an ambiguous cue, a duplicate, and a suspected false friend. Inspect stage records, evidence, decision paths, accepted output, wall-clock timing, and work per accepted card.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an ordinary low-risk candidate with aligned meaning, an unambiguous cue, no unresolved overlap or contradictory evidence, at least two independent claim-relevant signals, and independent verifier agreement, **When** its risk decision runs, **Then** it can be accepted without a redundant adversarial call, with every performed and skipped stage recorded.
2. **Given** a claim supported only by its generating AI response, **When** quality gates run, **Then** the candidate is held pending evidence and is absent from learner data; an affirmative defect may instead justify rejection.
3. **Given** credible evidence that both proposed words are normal in both languages, **When** a `different-common-word` claim is challenged, **Then** the relation is corrected if supported or withheld if unresolved.
4. **Given** a suspected false friend without strong independent agreement and evidence of a misleading meaning contrast, **When** its stricter gate runs, **Then** it is held unless an affirmative defect justifies rejection.
5. **Given** accepted, held, and rejected candidates, **When** a batch closes, **Then** its report reconciles all outcomes and only accepted entries are eligible for release.
6. **Given** a failed source retrieval or exhausted investigation budget, **When** the candidate is decided, **Then** missing evidence remains distinct from contradiction and the available evidence determines acceptance or hold; the failure alone never causes rejection.

---

### User Story 2 - Study the Initial Library (Priority: P1)

An Indonesian or Malaysian learner studies useful vocabulary across practical categories and cross-language relationships. A neutral English cue or image appears on the front; both language answers appear together on the back.

**Why this priority**: The process must produce actual learning content with varied vocabulary patterns.

**Independent Test**: Inspect the initial corpus and built deck for category and relation variety, aligned meanings, one card per ID, stable existing identities, and no answer or relation clue on the front.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** completed initial batches, **When** the learner source is inspected, **Then** it contains a useful range of accepted contrasts across practical categories and eligible relations; missing category or false-friend coverage is reported without promoting weak entries to meet a quota.
2. **Given** an accepted concept, **When** its card is studied, **Then** its front reveals neither answer nor relation, and its back shows Bahasa Indonesia and Bahasa Melayu answers together; a false-friend card also gives a brief explanation of the misleading lookalike and its actual meaning.
3. **Given** an existing concept whose wording or relation is corrected, **When** the deck is rebuilt, **Then** its permanent ID and note GUID remain unchanged and it still produces one card.
4. **Given** an identical ordinary vocabulary pair, **When** it is classified as `same`, **Then** it is rejected before expensive evidence and review calls and never appears in the learner TSV.

---

### User Story 3 - Grow and Audit Later Batches (Priority: P2)

A maintainer runs another topic batch from documented materials, checks overlap with the released library, and can revisit prior decisions and evidence.

**Why this priority**: Repeatability and duplicate control allow AI to remain the primary long-term growth mechanism.

**Independent Test**: Propose an existing concept under a new English synonym in a later batch and inspect duplicate handling, ID assignment, reporting, and provenance.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an existing concept proposed with a synonym, plural, or trivial form variation, **When** duplicate review runs, **Then** the proposal is merged, rejected, or explicitly distinguished before any new ID is assigned.
2. **Given** a prior hold or disputed rejection, **When** it is reevaluated under versioned rules, **Then** its candidate identity, prior evidence, conflicts, decisions, and prior model outputs remain linked; only invalidated stages rerun, and no new learner ID is assigned unless it passes the revised gate.
3. **Given** a completed batch, **When** the report is read, **Then** totals reconcile and relation distribution, duplicates, investigation needs, verification failures, accepted-card throughput, and available work-per-card measures are visible.
4. **Given** independent candidates in a batch, **When** research and verification run, **Then** bounded batching or concurrency can reduce wall-clock time while preserving claim-level provenance and safe retries.

### Edge Cases

- A dictionary confirms existence but does not establish preferred everyday usage.
- An English cue has multiple senses and the two answers correspond to different ones.
- A difference reflects register, region, age, or specialty rather than national usage.
- Independent checks disagree, a source is unavailable, or evidence contradicts the proposal.
- A lookalike has multiple meanings, only one of which resembles the other language's meaning.
- A later batch repeats a concept under a synonym, plural, or inflected form.
- Two similar candidates actually represent distinct learner meanings.
- A rejected candidate is proposed again; its prior decision is visible and no automatic acceptance occurs.
- An accepted entry is corrected after release; its ID stays stable unless it was a different semantic concept.
- A batch yields no accepted entries; the report still explains the outcome without weakening gates.
- Source retrieval fails, returns no evidence, or exhausts its budget without contradictory evidence; the candidate remains plausible and can be held for later recovery.
- A dictionary observation is relevant to meaning but not preference; contextual preference support from independent sources is evaluated without treating the dictionary-only observation as a veto.
- An older verifier uses `same` for shared meaning although the ordinary forms differ; taxonomy disagreement triggers versioned adjudication, not permanent loss of prior research.
- Cached evidence has the same publisher under multiple mirrors, or supports a lemma but not the new cue or register; reuse must preserve source independence and claim scope.
- Concurrent candidates reach duplicate screening or ID promotion together; one atomic promotion decision prevents duplicate permanent IDs and partial learner data.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Curation MUST use bounded topic-based batches and be repeatable from repository documentation and retained materials, without relying on an undocumented chat session. The batch design MUST support safe amortization of research and bounded concurrent work on independent candidates.
- **FR-002**: AI MUST perform the primary discovery, usage research, classification, verification, and expansion work. Routine high-confidence acceptance MUST NOT require human review of every item.
- **FR-003**: The curation process MUST separately identify generation, linguistic comparison, evidence checking, independent verification, conditional adversarial challenge, semantic duplicate review, risk/confidence assessment, and final decision. Generation MUST NOT approve its own output. Only stages applicable to the documented risk path MUST complete before acceptance; skipped stages MUST have a reason. A candidate finalized early as `hold` or `rejected` MUST retain the reason and statuses of uncompleted stages, without fabricating a result.
- **FR-004**: Curation MUST target contemporary common Indonesian in Indonesia and Malaysian Malay as commonly used in Malaysia, distinguishing existence, comprehension, common use, and preferred everyday use.
- **FR-005**: Each accepted concept MUST have exactly one contrast relation: `similar`, `different-common-word`, or `false-friend`, as defined below. `same` remains a candidate classification but MUST be rejected before promotion. Normal validation and build MUST reject a learner TSV row marked `same`.
- **FR-006**: A `different-common-word` decision MUST establish shared neutral meaning and useful, natural everyday preference in each language, rather than merely different dictionary translations, synonyms, register, or overgeneralized regional use.
- **FR-007**: Beyond the ordinary evidence gate, a `false-friend` decision MUST have independent evidence for each language's relevant meaning and for the misleading contrast, plus agreement from a verifier separate from generation and from mandatory adversarial review. Curation records MUST identify the misleading form, the language in which it misleads, and its actual meaning separately from the learner-facing contrast text. An unresolved disagreement MUST place it on hold; an affirmative disproof MAY justify rejection.
- **FR-008**: Each accepted ordinary candidate MUST have at least two independent, claim-relevant external sources or signals supporting its key meaning and usage claims. Evidence MUST be gathered during curation, be inspectable, and identify its source and supported claim. Previously inspected evidence MAY be reused for a new claim only when its observed content supports that precise cue, sense, locale, register, and claim; provenance and independence MUST remain visible. Mirrors or repeated pages of one underlying source count once. An AI-supplied citation or audit claim MUST NOT count until a separate source inspection records what was observed and how it was accessed. AI assertions do not count as external evidence, and dictionary existence alone MUST NOT establish everyday preference. A dictionary-only observation MUST NOT veto a preference claim independently supported by applicable contextual or comparative evidence.
- **FR-009**: Independent verification MUST challenge semantic alignment, common usage, register, regionality, alternate meanings, evidence independence, and relation classification for every accepted candidate. Adversarial review MUST challenge those issues for elevated-risk candidates, including false friends, verifier disagreement, material source conflict, ambiguous semantics, and disputed common-usage distinctions. Low-risk ordinary candidates MAY bypass adversarial review after independent verification agrees. An objection MUST name a concrete defect, conflicting observation, or material unresolved risk; generic hesitation or lack of perfect certainty alone MUST NOT veto acceptance. Material unresolved disputes block acceptance even when the minimum evidence count is met.
- **FR-010**: Confidence and risk decisions MUST consider verifier agreement, evidence quality, conflicts, semantic alignment, usage consistency, relation certainty, overlap, and source limitations. Unsupported model self-ratings MUST NOT be sufficient. The risk assessment MUST select and record the ordinary fast path or an escalation path before the final gate.
- **FR-011**: Every candidate in a closed batch MUST receive one deliberate final outcome: `accepted`, `hold`, or `rejected`. `accepted` means the applicable risk-tier evidence and verification gates pass with no unresolved material contradiction. `hold` means a plausible candidate has a material unresolved question, missing evidence, failed retrieval, disagreement, or exhausted research budget. `rejected` requires an affirmative exclusion basis such as confirmed semantic mismatch, incorrect translation, true duplicate, no learning contrast, materially misleading usage, or disproved false-friend claim. Model hesitation, timeouts, and budget exhaustion alone MUST NOT cause rejection. An interrupted or incomplete stage leaves a candidate pending until resumed or explicitly held with its reason and uncompleted-stage statuses. A batch with pending candidates MUST NOT close or change learner data; only accepted entries may enter learner data.
- **FR-012**: Duplicate review MUST compare candidates within a batch, across all batches being promoted together, against earlier candidate archives, and against the released library, including identical cues, singular/plural forms, trivial morphology, synonyms, and alternate English labels. Overlap MUST be merged, rejected, or explicitly distinguished before assigning a new ID.
- **FR-013**: English cues MUST identify one neutral concept shared by both answers, with concise sense clarification where needed and without unnecessary explanatory prose.
- **FR-014**: The learner source MUST remain a human-inspectable version-controlled vocabulary table with the implemented columns `id`, `english`, `indonesian`, `malaysian`, `relation`, `image`, `tags`, and `contrast`; image and tags MAY be empty. `contrast` MUST be concise and populated for each accepted false friend. Curation provenance MUST remain separate.
- **FR-015**: Permanent IDs MUST be assigned only after acceptance, remain unique and stable for the same concept, and alone determine Anki note identity. Curation metadata and other learner fields MUST NOT affect GUID derivation.
- **FR-016**: Curation records MUST retain batch or origin, stable candidate identity, proposed and final claims, stage outcomes, supporting references, material conflicts, disposition and reason, prompt/pipeline version, risk path, evidence-reuse lineage, and curation time sufficient for reevaluation. Candidate identity is the pair `(batch_id, candidate_id)`; candidate IDs MAY recur in different batches. Prior records MUST remain linked after policy recalibration; missing new fields in older records MUST be migrated, defaulted explicitly, or resolved through targeted reevaluation rather than deleting history. If a measured pilot runs in an isolated workspace, its accepted candidate records and provenance MUST be transferred through a validated, auditable handoff before canonical promotion, without importing pilot-only learner TSV or ID assignments. A changed decision after canonical recheck MUST be appended as a versioned revision linked to the preserved pilot decision, with its reason and report version.
- **FR-017**: Each batch MUST report generated, accepted, held, rejected, and pending counts; relation distribution; detected duplicates; investigation items; verification failures; path/tier distribution; and available accepted-card throughput and work-per-card metrics. Open reports MUST reconcile all five counts, including pending; closed reports MUST have zero pending and reconcile generated against the three final outcomes. Reports MUST distinguish historically accepted decisions from cards actually promoted into learner data.
- **FR-018**: The initial corpus MUST result from fully checked batches and provide useful contrast variety across practical categories and eligible relations. The former 24-card, three-category, three-relation target is a provisional planning estimate, not a mandatory promotion quota or a reason for unbounded research. Before initial-corpus completion, the measured pilot MUST establish a capacity-based release target using observed quality-adjusted throughput and report any coverage shortfall; no candidate may be accepted to meet that target.
- **FR-019**: Decision records MUST preserve enough information to later measure reported errors, relation misclassification, duplicate acceptance, correction rate, false-friend accuracy, false-negative recovery, hold/reject share, and reasons for path selection, without building learner feedback collection now.
- **FR-020**: Accepted entries MUST become ordinary deterministic source data before deck generation. Normal validation, local `.apkg` builds, and GitHub release builds MUST NOT invoke AI or live language research; after dependencies are available, validation and package generation MUST work offline.
- **FR-021**: Card fronts MUST use an English cue or image and the existing neutral prompt, revealing neither relation nor answer. Backs MUST show both labeled language answers; for a false friend, the back MUST also identify the misleading lookalike and its actual meaning in a concise evidence-backed contrast. One concept MUST remain one note and one card.
- **FR-022**: The learner-source schema change MUST receive a compatibility assessment and regression verification for permanent IDs, deck and note-model identities, GUID derivation, and any required migration before release.
- **FR-023**: Documentation MUST explain the AI-first rationale and fallibility, separate generation and verification, relation and acceptance rules, evidence and everyday usage expectations, risk-based adversarial handling, evidence reuse, bounded work, batch growth, neutral cues, throughput measurement, and conversion of accepted concepts to deterministic source data.
- **FR-024**: Ordinary low-risk candidates MUST have an optimized fast path with deterministic validation, semantic duplicate screening, at least two independent claim-relevant evidence signals, an independent verifier, and a final acceptance gate. A candidate that passes these conditions with no material risk signal MUST NOT require repeated review merely to reconfirm the same conclusion. Elevated-risk indicators MUST trigger bounded, targeted escalation; false friends always use their stricter gate.
- **FR-025**: Research, verifier, adversarial, conflict-resolution, and malformed-output retries MUST have configurable per-candidate or per-batch limits. At a limit, accept only if the applicable gate already passes, otherwise hold a plausible candidate or reject one with an affirmative exclusion basis. The pipeline MUST NOT pursue certainty recursively without a bound.
- **FR-026**: Curation MUST distinguish missing, unavailable, or insufficient evidence from evidence that materially contradicts a claim. Source retrieval failures MUST preserve retryable state and provenance, and MUST NOT become negative linguistic evidence. Concrete contrary evidence MUST be retained and considered by verification and escalation.
- **FR-027**: An evidence store or equivalent reusable record MUST retain source identity, observed passage or query, access time, claim scope, limitations, and reuse lineage. Reusing a source for multiple candidates MUST NOT multiply independent-signal counts or broaden its original meaning, register, locality, or sense. Related candidates MAY share retrieval, corpus queries, duplicate screening, or model verification while retaining candidate- and claim-level attribution.
- **FR-028**: Independent candidate work MUST support bounded concurrent execution where dependencies allow it. Measured curation runs MUST execute model calls through an instrumented invocation boundary that links each versioned packet, actual provider call, response, and candidate-stage record; packet-only/manual exchange MAY remain available but MUST NOT be represented as measured provider execution. Batching and parallelism MUST preserve deterministic duplicate and ID promotion, provenance, provider rate-limit handling, resumability, and no partial mutation of canonical learner data.
- **FR-029**: Existing held and potentially false-negative rejected candidates MUST have an explicit versioned reconsideration path that retains their original candidate IDs and links prior claims, evidence, role outputs, decisions, prompts, and batch state. The path MUST reuse valid completed work and rerun only invalidated stages; it MUST prevent a reassessed candidate from being mistaken for a new independent concept or assigned an accidental new permanent ID.
- **FR-030**: A small calibration set derived from existing work MUST contain accepted contrasts, suspected false negatives, affirmed rejects, duplicates, ambiguous cases, and elevated-risk or false-friend candidates. Comparative evaluation MUST test recovery of good candidates and continued exclusion of demonstrably bad ones, while stating sample-size limits and never treating the old pipeline decision as ground truth by default.
- **FR-031**: Curation MUST capture end-to-end batch wall-clock time from generation through successful promotion, path/tier timing, stage start/end or elapsed time, actual model calls, input/output tokens when provided, research operations, retries, escalation, and outcome. The elapsed interval MUST include provider queues, rate-limit waits, backoff, retries, evidence retrieval, handoff/recheck when applicable, and export. Only explicitly recorded periods when the workflow was externally paused between resumptions MAY be excluded; report both excluded time/reasons and total calendar span. A frozen-rubric evaluation audit performed between provisional export and canonical promotion MUST be logged as an evaluation pause and excluded from AI active curation time; any resumed research, correction, recheck, handoff, or export work MUST remain counted. Independent audit time MUST likewise be excluded from the expert's active curation denominator and reported separately. Audit findings MUST NOT silently approve or alter a curation decision. A model call without an instrumented invocation record MUST have unavailable call latency/usage rather than inferred values from response ingestion. External source research MUST have a start/finish operation record to count retrieval attempts or be explicitly reported with unavailable retrieval count/latency; inspected-source rows MUST NOT be treated as retrieval-operation counts. Reports MUST compute promoted accepted learner cards per active elapsed hour and work/cost per promoted card where data exists, separately for ordinary fast-path and escalated candidates. Isolated pilot exports MUST be labeled provisional benchmark output; only newly promoted cards in canonical learner data count toward product throughput. The final report MUST reconcile pilot-exported, canonically held/rejected, and canonically promoted candidate identities, and report the audited pilot cohort's canonical throughput separately from total newly promoted corpus counts. A whole-release cards/hour rate MUST be reported only if a complete active interval exists for every included batch or reconsideration, with concurrent intervals treated as one elapsed wall-clock window; otherwise its count remains visible and its rate MUST be labeled unavailable. Only canonically promoted cards whose final learner content and claim evidence match a passing frozen-rubric audit MAY enter a quality-adjusted numerator. A material post-audit change MUST invalidate that audit for metric use until the changed card is re-audited; an unaudited changed card, additional batch, or recovered historical card MAY count in raw corpus output but MUST NOT inflate a quality-adjusted numerator. Unknown data MUST be labeled unavailable, not estimated as fact.
- **FR-032**: A small representative expert-human benchmark MUST measure accepted cards per elapsed hour under comparable cue, relation, evidence, and linguistic-quality expectations. It is an evaluation baseline only, not routine review or a release approval gate. The AI-to-expert ratio MUST compare a prospectively designated ordinary-intent measurement lane with the ordinary expert benchmark. This lane MUST use a dedicated measured generation pass and run through canonical promotion without unrelated candidate work inside its active wall-clock interval. Its actual candidate IDs MUST freeze after generation and before research or decisions. A candidate later escalated or identified as a false friend MUST remain in the frozen lane and its elapsed interval; its accepted card MUST NOT enter the ordinary numerator, and its outcome and cost MUST be reported separately. Only audit-passing ordinary-fast canonical promotions from frozen lane candidates count in the ordinary numerator. Neither membership nor elapsed work MAY be removed after outcomes are known. If the lane is empty, its positive active elapsed time cannot be measured, or the expert accepted-card rate is zero or unavailable, the ratio MUST be undefined and no human-relative success claim made. A nonempty measured lane with zero ordinary-fast accepted cards MUST report AI throughput and the ratio as zero when the expert rate is positive. The long-term ordinary-fast-path objective is `AI accepted-card throughput / expert accepted-card throughput >= 50`; no assumed human cards-per-hour value or arbitrary intermediate improvement threshold may be introduced before measurement.
- **FR-033**: Quality evaluation MUST consider accepted-card throughput jointly with error findings, corrected relation classes, duplicate acceptance, evidence quality, hold/reject distributions, and recovered suspected false negatives. A rise in acceptance rate or strictness alone MUST NOT count as improvement. The mature ordinary fast path MUST handle routine eligible vocabulary normally; exceptional candidates MUST NOT impose their maximum-cost path on ordinary candidates. The eligible and escalated shares MUST be measured rather than assigned an arbitrary quota.

### Relation Rules

- **`same`**: The same or effectively identical form is commonly used for the intended concept in both languages. Classify and reject; this relation is never learner-facing source data.
- **`similar`**: Commonly used forms are recognizably related but differ enough for useful recall practice.
- **`different-common-word`**: Different words are normally preferred in everyday usage for the same neutral concept; dictionary possibility alone is insufficient.
- **`false-friend`**: A similar or identical-looking form may cause incorrect interpretation because its common meaning or usage differs significantly between the languages.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Candidate**: A proposed neutral concept and language pair tied to a batch, not yet eligible for release.
- **Evidence Record**: An inspectable reference linked to a particular meaning, usage, existence, or preference claim, including contradictions.
- **Curation Decision**: Stage results, relation, risk path, confidence rationale, final outcome, and reason.
- **Evidence Reuse Link**: A claim-scoped reference from a candidate to a previously inspected source observation, retaining publisher identity, limits, and prior usage lineage.
- **Reconsideration Record**: A versioned link from a historical candidate and decision to reevaluation under revised rules, preserving its identity and reusable stage artifacts.
- **Calibration Case**: A representative existing candidate with evidence and an independently reasoned evaluation label or unresolved status; an old pipeline outcome alone is not its truth label.
- **Efficiency Record**: Stage, path, batch, and promotion timing and work measures, with explicit missing-data markers for unavailable token or monetary cost fields.
- **Batch**: A bounded category-focused curation run with candidate records and an aggregate quality report.
- **Learner Concept**: An accepted neutral concept with permanent ID, English cue, both answers, one relation, optional image and tags, and a required concise back-side contrast when its relation is `false-friend`.
- **Deck Card**: The existing single-card learning unit built from a learner concept with stable identity.

### Dependencies and Scope

- This feature extends the existing version-controlled source and deterministic Anki build. Its implemented eight-column source contract and verified note-schema compatibility remain binding; curation policy changes MUST NOT change Anki GUID derivation.
- External research is a curation input. Source suitability depends on the claim; no single named dictionary, corpus, or provider is mandated.
- Community voting, formal reviewer roles, reputation, moderation UI, learner feedback collection, runtime AI in Anki, AI or network calls during build, automatic images, audio, pronunciation, and uncontrolled bulk generation are out of scope.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: For 100% of accepted entries in a completed batch, a reviewer can locate separate generation and independent verification records, claim-specific evidence, duplicate disposition, risk-path decision, applicable adversarial outcome or documented bypass, and an acceptance rationale; zero held or rejected entries enter learner data.
- **SC-002**: In a seeded quality exercise containing an ambiguous cue, unsupported usage preference, duplicate, unverified false friend, and evidence-retrieval failure, each is accepted only when its applicable gate passes, held when a material question remains, or rejected only for an affirmative exclusion reason. A missing source alone never produces rejection.
- **SC-003**: The initial library contains an inspectable, useful set of accepted contrasts across practical categories. The provisional 24-card and three-relation estimate is reported alongside actual coverage and shortfalls, without being used as a quota or as grounds for weaker gates or unbounded research. A measured pilot sets the capacity-based release target after baseline and quality evaluation; this criterion remains unmet until that target and the other Feature 003 success criteria pass.
- **SC-004**: Every open-batch report reconciles 100% of generated candidates across accepted, held, rejected, and pending counts. Every closed-batch report has zero pending candidates, reconciles 100% across accepted, held, and rejected outcomes, and includes all required quality measures.
- **SC-005**: In a later-batch duplicate exercise using an existing concept under another English label, zero accidental duplicate IDs are assigned, and the merge, rejection, or semantic distinction is recorded.
